# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## Welcome to the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Book

This comprehensive guide explores the cutting-edge field of humanoid robotics, where artificial intelligence meets physical systems to create machines capable of interacting with the world in human-like ways.

## What You'll Learn

This book is organized into four comprehensive modules:

1. **Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)** - Understanding the communication and control infrastructure that enables humanoid robots to function as integrated systems.

2. **Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)** - Creating virtual replicas of physical robots for simulation, testing, and development.

3. **Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)** - Implementing artificial intelligence for perception, planning, and control in humanoid systems.

4. **Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** - Developing systems that can perceive their environment, understand human commands, and execute complex actions.

## Prerequisites

To get the most out of this book, you should have:
- Basic programming experience (Python preferred)
- Understanding of linear algebra and calculus
- Familiarity with robotics concepts (helpful but not required)

## How to Use This Book

Each module builds upon the previous one, but can also be read independently based on your interests and needs. We recommend following the modules sequentially for a comprehensive understanding of humanoid robotics systems.

Let's begin our journey into the fascinating world of Physical AI and humanoid robotics!
